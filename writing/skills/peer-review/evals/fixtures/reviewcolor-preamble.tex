\title{Solvation Free Energies of Small Ions in Bulk Water: A Benchmark Study}
\author{Author et al.}
\date{2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Solvation free energies of monovalent ions are benchmark quantities for force field
development. We compute them for four ions with a thermodynamic integration protocol and
compare the values against a standard hydration series.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Ion solvation controls selectivity in separation membranes and electrolyte design. Benchmark
sets exist for monovalent ions, but the protocol details differ between studies.

\section{Methods}

Solvation free energies followed from thermodynamic integration in explicit water with a
three-point water model. Each ion was decoupled over twenty windows, and the integrals were
combined with the trapezoidal rule.

\section{Results}

The computed solvation free energies reproduce the hydration series to within 3 kJ mol-1 for
three of the four ions studied. The remaining ion deviates by 9 kJ mol-1, which is larger than
the integration uncertainty.

\section{Conclusions}

The integration protocol reproduces most of the hydration series, and the outlier marks the
ion whose parameters need recalibration.
