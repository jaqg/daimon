## Core idea
Density functional theory (DFT) reformulates the many-electron quantum problem in terms of
the electron density rather than the many-body wavefunction; the Kohn-Sham scheme replaces
the interacting system with a non-interacting one having the same ground-state density,
making accurate electronic-structure calculations tractable for large systems.
